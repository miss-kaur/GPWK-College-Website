Eligibility Norms for Admission to 1st year of Diploma in Pharmacy

The minimum eligibility qualification/criteria for admission to Diploma in Pharmacy course is pass in any of the following examination on the merit of marks obtained in Physics, chemistry. English and Mathematics/ Biology:

1. Intermediate examination in Science.

2. The First year of the Three-year degree course in Science.

3. 10+2 examination (academic stream) in Science.

4. Pre-Degree Examination.

5. Other qualification can be seen at following link.
