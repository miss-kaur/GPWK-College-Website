Overview

Diploma in Eltx. & Comm. Engineering is a diploma level study, application and control of the scientific phenomenon concerned with flow of electrical current. Telecommunication sector involves the telephone industry, computer industry, microwave radio systems, satellite communications, earth stations using satellite dishes, cellular phones industry.

In the recent years , Electronics and Communication has influenced the industrial growth in a massive way and made unprecedented growth in terms of new technologies, innovations and inventions all over the world to fulfill the need of the world or work. Our day to day life is surrounded by many Electronics gadgets today such as Computers, Mobile Phone, Laptops, Communication equipment’s and other gadgets like Television, CD player etc. The technological advancements with the each passing day in these areas have enriched our life in a great way and to cope with currently changing technological environment, it is mandatory to learn right from basics to diversified areas of electronics. Industrial market has high potential for highly skilled and scientifically oriented manpower, students, faculty & industrial staff need proper training in the recent technologies to improve productivity of organization.

Course at a glance

In the 5th and 6th Semester offers electives such as Medical Electronics, Advance Communication , Computer Networks, VLSI System Design , Advance Microprocessor etc. The content of this diploma programme prepares you for Industry oriented skill mastering ,which help to enhance your career prospects. A 4-week Industrial Training programme after 4th semester at reputable companies to deepen your skills is essential part of the diploma study scheme

You are eligible to take direct entry into the second year of the Engineering course(B.Tech.) under Lateral Entry Programme. Furthermore, this diploma has been given equivalence to 10+2 by the state board of School education also.
