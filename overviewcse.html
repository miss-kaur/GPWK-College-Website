Overview

Diploma in Computer Engineering is a diploma level technical qualification by acquiring which you can gain knowledge and skill sets for the emerging trends to develop smart digital solutions for innovative techno-info services in this era of digital world. Computer Engineering is a discipline about the hardware and software aspects of computer science. Computers are today at the heart of all those intelligent and high tech systems devices and by applying the skill sets achieved after undergoing Diploma in Computer Engineering one can make these devices and systems smarter and more intelligent.

During the study period of three years , you will be equipped with solid foundation in computer and electronic hardware design, software programming, networking, cyber security, and cloud computing, web designing, android development etc. You will master the skill of integrating various computing technologies to design and implement smart systems and robotics. These skills & knowledge will empower you to take up the challenges of this digital age.

Course at a glance

In the 5th and 6th Semester offers electives such as Cloud Computing, Android Programming, Web Technologies, Cyber Security etc. The content of this diploma programme prepares you for Industry Certification such as Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), Red Hat Certified Engineers (RHCE), Google Analytics etc. ,which help to enhance your career prospects. A 4-week Industrial Training programme after 4th semester at reputable companies to deepen your skills is essential part of the diploma study scheme

You are eligible to take direct entry into the second year of the Engineering course(B.Tech.) under Lateral Entry Programme. Furthermore, this diploma has been given equivalence to 10+2 by the state board of School education also.
