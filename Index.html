INSTITUTE AT A GLANCE

Govt. Polytechnic for Women, Kandaghat is the only polytechnic for women in the state of H.P.and was established in 1984. The main stress is to provide our society with quality technical education to women so that it can contribute towards the industrial development in the northern region. The Polytechnic has undergone a massive modernization and computerization under the World Bank assisted project and TEQIP project for technical education. All courses are approved by the All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE). The H.P.Takniki Shiksha Board, Dharmshala is the examination conducting body and conducts Polytechnic Admission Test (PAT) for admission to the Polytechnics in the state....

Vision and Mission

Vision of the Institute

"To be self-reliant as centre of excellence, to provide world class technical education and training to women,to provide service to industry and community, to contribute towards self empowerment of women and in the growth of our economy."

Mission of the Institute

The institute is committed to provide trained technical manpower to the unorganised, service and rural sectors as well, to assist in the national effort to bring about uniform and sustainable upliftment in all spheres of national economy. In this institute, we strive for the total personality development of the students, unfolding their leadership qualities so that in addition to machines and materials,they can also supervise and manage the workforce in the industries. In the fast changining scenario, the students coming from different target groups, environment and socio-economic background bring varrying expectations, values and interests which has made the institutional administration extremely challenging. These emerging situations are brining continuous changes in the goals and objectives of this institute.

Quality Policy

"At Govt. Polytechnic for Women, Kandaghat, we are committed to :

a) Continually improve technical education and technological development with innovative methods and their effective implementation for
• Training and empowerment of women
• Service to industry and community
• Growth of National Economy.
• Value addition to interested party requirements.
b) To comply with requirements and continually improve effectiveness of the Quality Management System.

Principal's Message

It is a matter of great pleasure for me to introduce you to the techno-edu scenario of GPW Kandaghat.The fast changing techno, socio-economic and Industrial Development through globalisation in most parts of the world has resulted in a greater demand for quality technical manpower. Govt. Polytechnic for Women, Kandaghat, Distt. Solan, Himachal Pradesh was established in the year 1984 with aim of providing high quality education in Engineering and Technology and meet the requirements of present world of work. This is the only Institute in Himachal Pradesh providing technical education to the women in the state. The CAMPUS is spread over 6.40 acres of the land. The Institute is situated 16 KM from SOLAN on the National Highway CHANDIGARH-KALKA-SHIMLA and is 30 KM from Capital SHIMLA. It is an old Palace of MAHARAJA PATIALA and is surrounded by unique natural surrounding. During the last three decades of its existence, the Institute has made tremendous progress in the field of providing technical education particuarly to the women from all over the state of H.P. and adjoining states. It is the first ISO-9001:2008 Certified Polytechnic for all Courses.

Er. Vinita Arya

Principal

Location

This Polytechnic is situated on NH-22 (Shimla-Kalka highway) and is 30 KM from Shimla and 90 KM from Chandigarh. The Institute can also be reached by weekly Delhi-Shimla flight as well as through narrow gauge run Kalka-Shimla train connected to Delhi-Kalka Shatabadi Express. The campus is spread over a land of 6.40-acres. The Institute has spacious,ventilated and well furnished lecture halls, well equipped labs and workshops. There are three girls hostel in the campus. Campus is fully residential and has all amenities. The institute has dedicated and experienced faculty. The campus is spread over lush green land and surrounded by beautiful hills having good climate throughout the year.
